Queues · Wind Breathing First Form

A queue is a linear data structure that follows the FIFO (First-In-First-Out) principle. Similar to air currents flowing sequentially through a cave, the first wind current to enter (Front) is the first to emerge/exit.

Breathing Technique Mechanics

  • Enqueue: Align a new form slice at the Rear of the queue pipeline.
  • Dequeue: Discharge the oldest style waiting at the Front of the queue.
  • Peek: View the next style in line at the Front without dequeuing.
  • FIFO Order: Orderly sequential execution — perfect for buffer queues.
